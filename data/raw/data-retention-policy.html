Data Retention Policy

Customer Document Storage

Uploaded customer documents are retained for the lifetime of the customer's active subscription. Upon subscription cancellation, documents and their associated embeddings are deleted within 30 days, and this deletion is irreversible after it completes.

Query Logs

Query logs, including the question text and returned citations, are retained for 180 days for debugging and quality-evaluation purposes. Query logs are anonymized after 30 days by stripping any customer-identifying metadata, but the question text itself is not redacted.

Embeddings Cache

Embeddings are cached indefinitely for deduplication purposes, keyed by a hash of the source text rather than the source document, so a cached embedding is not itself tied to a specific customer after the source document is deleted.
