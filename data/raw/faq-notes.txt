Internal FAQ notes, collected from support tickets.

Q: Why does the /v1/query endpoint sometimes return fewer than top_k citations?
A: If retrieval confidence for some candidate chunks falls below the internal
threshold, they are dropped from citations even if they were part of the
top_k retrieved set. This is intentional to avoid citing weakly relevant
chunks just to fill the requested count.

Q: Can customers change the dense/sparse fusion weighting themselves?
A: Not yet through the public API. It is configurable per workspace but
currently requires a platform team member to change it via the internal
admin tool. A self-serve setting is planned for a future release.

Q: What happens if a document fails indexing three times?
A: It is moved to a dead-letter queue and flagged in the customer's dashboard
with error code UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT or a generic indexing failure message,
depending on the failure reason.
